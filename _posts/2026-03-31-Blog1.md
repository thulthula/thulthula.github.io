The Base Architecture of current AI LLMs:
the current LLMs are basically a large set of numbers, one can imagine it as a excel file filled with numbers, billions and billions (or even trillion in some cases) of numbers, thats it, as joked by elon musk that our digital god is just a csv file?. 

Those numbers are called the 'weights' of the model. Whenever we hear that model is being trained or AI is being trained, we are just talking about the process of acquiring these numbers. Now how can be these numbers be interpreted as the info in the real world?
Lets understand this with the help of an example: Lets say our model essentially indentifies a given image as dog or a cat. This model (large excel of numbers) can be thought of as a big function which is applied over that image? How? An image is basiclly a set of numbers for a computer, each pixel has its pixel values which tells the intensity of the led and they are arranged in a grid. 

So this function(f) basically maps those image 'numbers' to either zero for cat or one for dog:

F([12,213,23,123],...)

Now how is it able to do that? The answer lies in the training or learning of those numbers. In the training process this model is presented with thousands of cat and dog images, and trained with a algorithm called as backpropogation. This feild of ai is filled with these bizz words. It is a simple idea, all those weights (which were initailed with random numbers) were changed one by one so that the function outputs the label dog when presented with a dog image and vice versa for a cat image. Now this classifier can be easily made with around 10k parameters, but imagine changing those billions and billions of numbers one by one so that those LLMs become accurate, and this is the reasons it costs million or even billions of dollars to train these models.

I would like to point to statement which we will discuss later that: A human child didn't need those 10k images before able to confidently identify a dog or a cat?
