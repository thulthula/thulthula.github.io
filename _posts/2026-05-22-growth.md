So yeah, that is a solid mindset that i can acquire, Now i present my mindset and how is it different from that:

My mindset started with the quote from art teacher:
>if you get satisfied, there wont be any growth.

This stuck with me and created a structure of growth fuled by unsatisfaction. 

It was a unsatisfaction fueled growth mechanism, i.e that is i set goals, and when i reach them ill set new bigger goals and stay in the loop of unsatisfaction. Also i set goals so high that i may not meet them, so i again be unsatisfied, so there's that also, For example: build a very big modern house on at least an Acre sq feet plot with mountain view, and have amg and aston martin.

Pros:
Fast Growth, Doesn't require much arguing with oneself for work being done

Cons:
- May lead to shallowness: in life and in work
- The exit

The exit strategy and the **problem** with this:

The exit strategy is when ill be old enough, like 40 or max 50, when ill be running behind some target and start to feel unsatisfied, there will come a day (which is speculative), when ill say stop atul, you have done enough, you should enjoy the fruits collected. 

And my bar of goals/greatness/satisfaction will take a free fall, and i will start to appreciate everything and my life, my growth run and everything ive done, ill ACTUALLY SEE now how far i've come and so on. 

So far so godd, but my realization is: i will feel that i should have dropped that bar wayyy before like 20-25 years before, and actually feel happy in the journey and everyday, have smile along the way instead of misery and pain.

I should have chased A TRUE NORTH STAR of ACTUALLY LIVING A FULFILLED LIFE, A HAPPY LIFE all along a way, instead of a OASIS STAR that i have created which surely lead to very focused fast but SAD AF growth.
I feel that instead of chasing that oasis and fake star i should try to be be happy and try my best of whatever i do, shape however and jitna i can, and let the life unfold and observe and adapt, without sacrificing my inner peace and happiness.

A TRUE CURIOUSITY DRIVEN APPOACH TO GROWTH. searching the WHY behind things instead of JUST HOW to achieve that thing. All of this realization should not act as reason or proxy for not working ya start being lazy, instead it should promote interacting with the world more and more with a new prespective
