Ok buckle up motherfuckers as this is going to be one of the most important writing of my life, here i am going to decide what my Growth mindset is going to be. 

What the fuck happened and what triggered this?

Backstory i was watching a interview of [Alysa Liu](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FATY5l5dku0) where she said a line which struck me crazy 4am in the middle of the night and i froze, the line was:
>let's say i didnt win this world championship today, i feel like things would be the same, if anything i would be less busy and i would have time to do other things that i wanted to do, so you know i dont really care where my life takes me because ill enjoy any road. I am pretty easily entertained, as long as my friends and family is with me and i can do fun stuff, and i can drink like really tasty water and eat real good food, i am happy

I realized that lately i am absolutely not like this and i think i know why, the "problem" is my growth mindset model.

-Like, there's not many things in life that you do where you have to give 100% of you, you know what I mean? And I kind of love feeling like I'm on my last breath, I guess, or I physically cannot do more. I love pushing myself to that boundary. And I guess I was just missing that when I quit because life was so easy. You know, you don't have to push your body to its limits, and I. I love that feeling because you need to have willpower for it.

-Obviously I had already been planning to quit for a long time. And I thought, well, if I can at least, you know, go to the Olympics and, you know, accomplish my childhood dream, because I also didn't want to feel like I did all that for nothing because I didn't enjoy, you know, much of the journey either. -But Now, I actually love the journey the most. Now, I, I mean, I like competitions too, <u>but my favorite part is getting to train every day, because that's kind of why I'm doing it again</u>. You know, I just want to use all of my energy.<u> It's so fun</u>.

I would never do that again. Like making sacrifices for something I didn't think was worth it.
